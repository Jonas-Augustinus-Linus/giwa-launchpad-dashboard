DEX / AMM specification candidate

Purpose: evaluate a valueless local and GIWA Sepolia liquidity experiment. It must not be presented as an official GIWA DEX or used for real-asset trading.

Design questions to resolve

  • Build versus integrate: select only a license-compatible, maintained, independently audited AMM base; do not copy the discovered fixed-pair prototype.
  • Pool model and invariant; fee accounting; protocol-fee authority; minimum liquidity; rounding; fee-on-transfer and non-standard token behavior; reentrancy and callback surface.
  • Router slippage/deadline protections; deterministic pair creation; LP token mechanics; reserve synchronization; donation and skim behavior; TWAP manipulation and oracle separation.
  • Admin roles, upgrades, pause behavior, multisig/timelock, monitoring, emergency exit, and an explicit no-custody/no-hidden-control statement.
  • GIWA sequencing and Flashblocks assumptions. Preconfirmation is not finality and the absence of a public L2 mempool does not eliminate ordering, sandwich, or sequencer risks.

Required evidence before a testnet deployment proposal

  • Unit, differential, invariant, stateful fuzz, adversarial-token, and fork-independent local tests; gas bounds and denial-of-service analysis.
  • Pinned source revision, compiler and dependencies; clean-room license review; reproducible bytecode; static analysis and independent audit.
  • Human-reviewed deployment parameters and roles; verified explorer source; post-deploy invariant checks; transparent testnet-only UI; no automated liquidity or public purchase invitation.
