02 — Cross-platform launch-structure norms (supply split, initial LP, graduation, dev buy, sniper controls)

Prepared 2026-09-06 (KST). Observation window: 2026-09-05 19:40–21:30 UTC unless a cell says otherwise. Lens: what a creator launching on an existing launchpad should expect the launch to look like, and what the market now reads as "clean". Not legal/tax/investment advice. 0. Direct answers (read this first)

Q1. "Of 100% supply, do you launch 80% or 90%?"

Neither number is a universal norm; the honest answer is "it depends on the venue type, and the choice is usually made for you":

  • Curve launchpads (pump-style) put a fixed share on the curve and hold the rest back for the graduation pool. Observed today: pump.fun 79.31% curve / 20.69% LP (official constants); Raydium LaunchLab/letsbonk 80% default (creator may pick 51–80%); Four.meme ~80/20; Bags-on-Robinhood 83/17 (830M/170M); Pons V2 (Robinhood) 71.43% sold / 28.57% held back, of which 20.41% actually lands in the pool and ~8.2% is locked as leftover. So the pump-style band is 71–83% on the curve, 17–29% reserved for LP, and none of it is the creator's to allocate — the reserved share is protocol-fixed, immutable and locked.
  • Direct single-sided pool launches (Clanker-style; Pons V1, o1, NOXA Fun, HoodPump on Robinhood) put 100% of supply into one locked concentrated-liquidity position at a starting tick. There is no "80 vs 90" decision: the only supply that leaves the pool is what buyers (including the creator's own dev buy) purchase. The one optional carve-out is a vault (Clanker: ≤30% in v3.1, no cap in v4; observed sample: 2% or 5% with 7-day lock).
  • Team allocations are the exception, not the norm. Of 15 venues surveyed, only Zora Creator Coins (50% vesting 5 years), Zora Content Coins (1% to creator), Raydium LaunchLab custom mode (vesting ≤30%), Clanker vault, and Flaunch premine (≤ fair-launch share) allow one. pump.fun, letsbonk JustSendIt, Bags (both chains), Pons V1/V2, o1, NOXA, HoodPump, Four.meme, Virtuals give the creator 0% at t0.

So: if you are asked "80 or 90", the market's answer is "0% to me, 100% to the curve/pool (minus a disclosed dev buy)"; where a curve exists, the 80/20-ish split is the platform's, not yours.

Q2. "What is the average initial LP in ETH?"

  • Creator-seeded ETH in a single-sided launch is 0 ETH by design on every EVM venue surveyed (Clanker v3.1/v4, Flaunch, Pons V1/V2, o1, NOXA Fun, HoodPump, Bags-RH). The ETH side of the pool is filled by buyers. What you control is the starting market cap (Clanker sample: 1.99 ETH ≈ $4.9k for 50% of recent deployments, 9.87 ETH ≈ $24.5k for 40%; o1 ≈ $4,000 FDV; Flaunch default $10,000; Pons V2 1.68 ETH ≈ $4.2k phantom reserve) and, optionally, your dev buy.
  • Real ETH that lands in the pool at graduation on Robinhood: Pons V2 = 4.2 ETH (+204.08M tokens; median of 1,351 graduations in the last ~2 days is exactly 4.2 ETH), Bags-RH = 5 ETH (+170M tokens), Pons V1 / NOXA Fun = 4.2 ETH "paired" milestone (no migration; the pool simply reaches 4.2 ETH of buyer ETH). Solana equivalents: pump.fun ≈ 85 SOL (≈ $8.8k), LaunchLab/letsbonk 85 SOL, Bags-Solana 85 SOL. BNB: Four.meme ≈ 18 BNB (≈ $13.9k). Virtuals: 42,000 VIRTUAL (≈ $28.9k).
  • So the "initial LP" a graduated memecoin carries today is ~$8–15k of quote asset on Solana/Robinhood/BNB (pump.fun 85 SOL ≈ $8.8k; Pons 4.2 ETH ≈ $10.4k; Bags-RH 5 ETH ≈ $12.4k; Four.meme 18 BNB ≈ $13.9k), against ~17–29% of supply, which prices the graduation market cap at ≈ $42k (pump.fun) / $51k (Pons V2) / $73k (Bags-RH) / ~$70k (Four.meme, derived).

Q3. "How are launchpad launches usually composed?" See §3 (d). One-line version: fixed supply 1B (100B on Clanker/Flaunch) → 100% to curve or pool, 0% team → creator pays a tiny fixed launch fee (0–0.02 ETH) → optional atomic dev buy of ~0.5–3% of supply → protocol-fixed graduation into a permanently locked pool → creator paid from a 0.5–1% share of trade fees in the quote asset, claimable, splittable.

A launch that keeps 10–20% "for the team" is outside today's norm on every memecoin venue surveyed; if you want inventory, the venues' own answer is buy it in the launch transaction, disclosed (see (c)).

(b) Initial LP in ETH for direct-pool launches on Base / Robinhood

  • Creator-seeded ETH = 0 on Clanker v3.1/v4, Flaunch, Pons V1, o1, NOXA Fun, HoodPump, Bags-RH, Pons V2. The contract refuses configurations that would require the creator to deposit ETH (o1: "If a configuration would require ETH … from the creator, the transaction stops"). The pool's ETH side is 100% buyer money.
  • Starting market cap is the real knob. Observed values: o1 ≈ $4,000 FDV (factory-managed); Clanker recent sample 1.99 ETH ≈ $4.9k (50%) or 9.87 ETH ≈ $24.5k (40%); Flaunch default $10,000; Pons V2 phantom 1.68 ETH ≈ $4.2k; Bags-RH 1.23 ETH ≈ $3.1k; pump.fun 28 SOL ≈ $2.9k. The band is $3k–$5k for "degen" starts and $10k–$25k for "serious" starts.
  • What the first buys look like (single-sided v3 position from tick t₀ upward): each buy moves price along the range; with 1B supply at $4k FDV the first 0.1 ETH ($248) buys ≈ 6% of supply on Clanker-style full-range math, which is why every Robinhood pad added a 3–20-second 99% tax or a per-wallet cap (Pons V1: ≤5.5% per wallet in blocks 1–2). On Pons V2 the empirical first-block buy is 0.001–0.125 ETH (p25–p90), i.e. $2.5–$310, taking 0.06–6.9% of supply.
  • "Graduated" ETH depth on Robinhood is protocol-fixed at 4.2 ETH (Pons V1/V2, NOXA) or 5 ETH (Bags-RH) ≈ $10.4k–$12.4k, paired with ≈17–20% of supply, giving a $51k–$73k graduation FDV. That is the number a Robinhood memecoin needs to reach for its pool to be "real"; below it, the token is still a curve position or a thin single-sided range.

(c) How much ETH a creator commits (dev buy) and how it is perceived

  • Mechanism norm: every venue that cares about snipers exposes an atomic dev buy (pump.fun creator buy in the create tx; LaunchLab "initial buy amount"; Four.meme same-tx purchase; Bags createAndBuy; Pons V2 launchAndBuy router; Pons V1 block-0 creator-only buy; o1 "Include Dev Buy" toggle; NOXA "Initial Buy"; Clanker ClankerUniv4EthDevBuy; Flaunch premine). The atomic buy is exempt from the venue's sniper tax (Pons V2 "the creator is never a sniper on their own launch"; o1 "pays the normal 1% but not the anti-snipe surcharge"). Raydium's official docs list "Not making an initial buy" as a mistake ("sniping bots may capture all early tokens at near-zero cost, which can undermine community trust").
  • Size norm (observed on Robinhood, Pons V2, 09-05): median 0.02 ETH ≈ $50 ≈ 1.2% of supply; the upper quartile is 0.05–0.13 ETH (2.9–6.9%); the largest in 40 was 0.45 ETH (21%). Bags-RH docs' worked example is 0.01 ETH. Clanker's recent Base deployments are bot-spam with dust dev buys, so they say nothing about human norms. Solana pump.fun "1–5% normal, ≥10% red flag" is community lore that I could not source to a primary page this session — treat as UNVERIFIED.
  • Perception heuristics the trenches actually use (sourced): GMGN's security panel marks Top-10 holders < 30% as "relatively safe", shows Burnt/Locked LP %, Insider holder ratio (wallets holding without buying = suspected creator split), Snipers / first-70-buyers total bought vs. current holdings, "Rug probability" from holder history, and flags a 99% sell tax as honeypot (CA security check, insiders/snipers, 09-05). Pons V1 hard-codes the same intuition as a rule: no wallet may hold >5% or buy >5.5% in blocks 1–2. Read together: a disclosed dev buy of ≤5% in one wallet is inside every heuristic; 5–10% is tolerated if disclosed and not split across wallets (splitting is exactly what the "insider" and "bundle" detectors are built to catch); >10% in the creator's hands is read as a dump risk regardless of intent.
  • Perceived meaning of the ETH amount: on Robinhood/Base, ~0.05–0.1 ETH ($125–$250) signals "creator has skin in the game, not a bot"; ~0.5 ETH+ ($1.2k+) buys 10–20% at a $4k start and is read as a pre-mine by another name unless the venue's per-wallet caps prevent it. On Solana the same logic is denominated in ~0.5–5 SOL.

(d) What a "clean" composition looks like to the current market

The pattern that every 2026 venue converged on, which the owner should copy whether launching on Pons V2, Bags-RH, o1, NOXA or a DIY v4 hook:

  1. Fixed supply, no mint/blacklist/pause/tax hooks (Pons V2: "no mint, no way to freeze or blacklist a wallet, no way to add a tax after launch"; o1: "no owner who can mint, pause, upgrade, or take balances"). Four.meme "Tax Tokens" and Virtuals' 1% tax are the outliers and are read as taxes.
  2. 0% team allocation at t0; 100% to curve/pool. Any creator inventory comes from a single, disclosed, atomic dev buy of roughly 1–5% of supply (≈0.02–0.1 ETH at Robinhood starting caps), from the same wallet that receives fees.
  3. Liquidity permanently locked or burned by the protocol, not by a promise (pump.fun LP burnt; LaunchLab locked/burned; Pons "the function does not exist"; o1 "Removal: permanently disabled"; NOXA "locked forever"; Flaunch "effectively burned"; Virtuals 10-year stake). On a DIY launch the market substitutes UNCX lock or an NFT burn for this.
  4. Creator monetised by a fee stream, not by supply: 0.5–1% of volume in the quote asset (Pons V2 0.7% of volume; o1 0.5%; Bags 1%; pump.fun 0.30%→0.05%; Clanker 80% of a 1–3% LP fee), pull-claimed, splittable to team/KOL wallets by bps (Bags ≤100 claimers, Clanker ≤7, pump.fun ≤10). Optional creator tax (Pons ≤10%) exists but every doc tells buyers to "read it before trading" — using it costs trust.
  5. Sniper control that the venue applies, not one you improvise: 99%→~0 tax over 3–20 s (Pons V2, o1, Zora), per-wallet caps in the first blocks (Pons V1), 30-min fixed price (Flaunch), or gas auctions/2-block delay (Clanker). Tax proceeds flow to the fee split or platform, never to the creator's wallet directly (Virtuals routes them to a vested team buyback, which is the one design that puts sniper tax in the team's pocket).
  6. Protocol-fixed graduation (4.2–5 ETH / 85 SOL / 18 BNB) into a public pool at the curve's end price; no creator step, no migration the creator can botch or skip. "Graduation is not a quality signal" is now boilerplate on Pons/NOXA.
  7. Metadata immutability + verifiable addresses (CREATE2 vanity/predictable addresses on Pons V2; profile immutable by default on o1).

Anything outside this — kept supply, mutable fees, creator-controlled LP, undisclosed multi-wallet buys — is precisely what GMGN/Rugcheck-style panels are built to flag, and on Robinhood the two largest pads (Pons V2, Bags) make most of it impossible by construction.

  • Robinhood Chain today: the volume is on Pons V2 (curve → v4, 4.2 ETH, 1% fee, 70% creator, 0.0005 ETH launch, 99% snipe tax 3 s, ~25k launches/day, 2.6% graduate), then Bags-RH (curve → v4, 5 ETH, 2% fee, 50% creator, creation fee currently 0), then the single-sided v3/v4 pads (o1 $4k start, 1% fee, 50% creator, 0.001 ETH; NOXA Fun v3 1%, 4.2 ETH milestone; Pons V1 v3 1%, 70% creator; HoodPump dormant).
