0. Executive summary (what to do, in order)

  1. Fund from a CEX to Arbitrum One, then Relay/Across into chain 4663. No CEX I could check (Binance, Coinbase) withdraws directly to Robinhood Chain; the Robinhood app itself does transfer ETH to Robinhood Chain but only for US accounts.   2. Wallet: Rabby (Robinhood Chain is in its native chain list) or MetaMask with manual params (chain 4663, RPC https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com, symbol ETH, explorer robinhoodchain.blockscout.com).
  3. Gas is trivial: a Pons V2 token creation costs ≈0.0015 ETH ($3.6) in gas + 0.0005 ETH ($1.24) launch fee; a fee claim costs ≈0.000013 ETH ($0.03). Gas price ≈0.4–0.5 gwei normally, spikes to ~5 gwei.
  4. Launch on Pons V2 (ponsfamily.com/launchpad/create; factory 0x7eD598Bc…EC7e). On-chain terms right now: 1 B fixed supply, 1% trading fee (70% creator / 30% protocol, or 35/35/30 with buyback on), optional creator tax up to 10% on top, 100% to creator, fixed at creation, graduation at 4.2 ETH raised (~$10.4k; ≈$51k FDV), permanent locked Uniswap-v4 liquidity, 99%→0 snipe tax over the first 3 s (contract) / 5 s (docs). Public launching is open (canLaunch(anyAddress) == true, launchEnabled == true), despite a stale "whitelist only" sentence in the docs.
  5. Competition is brutal and graduation is a launch-time decision: ~40,850 V2 launches and 959 graduations in the 36.6 h before observation (≈26.8k launches/day, 2.3% graduation rate); sampled graduations happen a median 2.0 min after launch (p90 41 min, none > 2 h) — the 4.2 ETH curve is bought out by the launcher's bundle + bots, not by an organic climb. 41% of graduated tokens set a creator tax (1–10%); none use buyback. Distribution, not deployment, is the whole game.
  6. Attention lives on GMGN (Robinhood tab; 1% fee; $2.8M/day fees on this chain), Dexscreener (chain slug robinhood; 16 of the 30 latest paid boosts and 21 of 30 latest token profiles were Robinhood-chain tokens at observation), X (@ponsdotfamily, @RobinhoodCrypto), hoodscan.co, and in Korea GMGN Korea TG (1,736 members) + dcinside 차트갤/코박갤 + fmkorea 코인게시판 + Naver blogs.

4.3 Curve economics (computed from config 0)

  • Reserved-for-pool = supply × phantom ÷ (phantom + threshold) = 1e9 × 1.68/5.88 = 285.7M tokens (28.57%); 714.3M sold on the curve.
  • Start price 1.68e-9 ETH/token → FDV at launch ≈ 1.68 ETH ≈ $4.2k. Graduation price 2.058e-8 ETH → FDV at graduation ≈ 20.6 ETH ≈ $51k, a 12.25× from first buy.
  • At graduation the 4.2 ETH raised + 285.7M tokens seed a Uniswap v4 pool (≈$20.8k two-sided liquidity), permanently locked (docs: "liquidity is locked permanently… no way for anyone to reach the liquidity once it is locked"). Same price on both sides — "every launch on the same settings graduates into a pool of the same size, at the same price."
  • If the curve is bought out by one buy, the buyer is filled up to the reserved allocation and refunded the rest.

4.4 Fees & creator income

  • Base fee 1% on every trade, same rate before and after graduation ("You pay the same rate whether the token is on its curve or in its Uniswap pool"); post-graduation the hook charges it, the v4 pool fee is 0, and "trading a graduated pons token does not cost you a Uniswap fee on top of the pons one."
  • Split (DefiLlama adapter constants, consistent with docs' "pons takes its share first"): protocol 30%; creator 70% with buyback off; with buyback on: 35% creator / 35% buyback. Buyback-bought tokens vest over five years to the creator (docs "Buyback and vesting").
  • Creator tax: optional, ≤10%, set at creation, immutable, charged on top of the 1%, 100% to the creator ("The creator tax… goes entirely to them"). Snipe tax is capped so the buyer always nets ≥1% of spend.
  • Payouts are in the launch's quote asset (ETH). Fees accrue in the Fee Escrow; creator calls claim() (native) or claimToken(addr) (ERC-20); read balanceOf(). In the UI this is the Profile page (ponsfamily.com/profile). Cost ≈$0.03 per claim (observed).
  • Creator can (a) setCreatorFeeRecipient / propose transferCreatorFeeRecipient (3-day timelock + 3-day execution window) and (b) toggle buyback — "Exactly two things stay adjustable." Community takeover (CTO) form: https://forms.gle/JjrWvybFeNfE5v8F6 (docs).
  • Worked example: a token that only graduates (4.2 ETH of curve buys) earns the creator 0.7% × 4.2 = 0.029 ETH ($73) from the base fee — plus 10% × 4.2 = 0.42 ETH if you set a 10% tax (which also deters buyers). Real income comes from post-graduation volume: 1 ETH creator income requires ≈143 ETH ($354k) of traded volume at 0.7%.

Reading: "good launches" do not climb to graduation over hours — the 4.2 ETH (~$10.4k) curve is bought out within minutes, usually by the launcher's own bundle plus early bots. A token that has not graduated within ~1 hour almost never does (0 of 22 sampled graduations took longer than 97 min, while 97.7% of launches never graduate at all). Creator settings in the same sample (read via getLaunchedToken): creator tax set on 9 of 22 (41%) graduated tokens — values 1%, 1.5%, 2%, 8%, 10% (median 0%); buyback enabled on 0 of 22. So a moderate tax (1–2%) is normal among tokens that graduate; nobody uses the buyback option.
