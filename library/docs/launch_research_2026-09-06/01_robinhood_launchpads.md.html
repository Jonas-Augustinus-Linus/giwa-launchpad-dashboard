Robinhood Chain launchpad landscape — creator's view (observed 2026-09-06 KST ≈ 2026-09-05 20:00–21:00 UTC)

Lens: which launchpads on Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663) have meaningful activity today, exactly how each one splits supply / fees / liquidity, and which one an experienced memecoin creator should use first to earn creator fees. GIWA is out of scope here except where a venue is dual-deployed (GIWA MARKETS).

Source classes: official (protocol docs / verified contracts), onchain (read via https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com and robinhoodchain.blockscout.com / robinscan.io, both Blockscout), third_party (DefiLlama API, GeckoTerminal API, DexScreener API, DefiLlama adapter source). ETH price used for USD conversions: $2,476 (Blockscout /api/v2/stats coin_price, 2026-09-05). Anything not directly observed is marked UNVERIFIED. Web search budget was exhausted before this task; everything below comes from direct fetches and on-chain reads.

0. TL;DR and ranked recommendation

  1. Pons V2 (ponsfamily.com/launchpad) — use first. It is where the chain's memecoin flow is: DefiLlama fees $8.75M/24h (2nd on the chain after Uniswap V4's $8.82M), $161M curve volume/24h, 7d $720M, 30d $872M, and daily fees grew from $254k (08-22) to $8.75M (09-04). On-chain last 24h: 26,711 curve launches and 708 graduations (~2.65%). Mechanics are the most creator-favourable of the large venues: 1% fee on curve and after graduation with 70% to creator (35% if you switch on buyback), plus an optional creator tax up to 10% on curve trades that goes 100% to the creator, 0.0005 ETH launch fee, 99%→0 anti-snipe tax in the first seconds that is paid into your fee pool, atomic launch-and-buy router, pull-claim escrow, and a public app with Profile/Analytics. Graduation at 4.2 ETH (or 8,090 USDG, or stock-token pairs) is low, so a launch reaches a real Uniswap v4 pool at ≈$51k FDV.
  2. Bankr (bankr.bot; contract LongLauncher, hook 0x4e34…a544) — second, for reach and the creator allocation. ~2,315 pools/day were created through its hook in the last 24h (second-highest count on the chain), it produced the two largest "new" tokens of the week (LIGERCOIN/LIT, amc/MEME) and 15% of supply vests to the creator. Creator gets 0.665% of volume (of a 1.75% total fee). Launch by tagging @bankrbot on X, via the Bankr terminal, or CLI. Not indexed by DefiLlama on Robinhood yet, so market data is GeckoTerminal-only.
  3. o1 Launchpad (launch.o1.exchange) — third, specifically for stock-paired launches. 1% fee, creator 50% / platform 30% / referrer 20%, 0.001 ETH creation fee, single-sided permanent v4 liquidity opening at ≈$4k FDV, 20-second 99%→1% anti-snipe, referral links you can farm yourself; 337 launches/day; produced FATCOIN ($29M) and NUDES ($24M) paired to LLY / SNAP stock tokens. Fees $236k/24h on Robinhood.
  4. Bags on Robinhood pays the best creator rate (1% of volume, 2% total) and has the cleanest fee-split tooling (up to 100 claimers), but has almost no flow ($9k fees/24h, ~24 factory events/24h) and no Robinhood UI I could find — only for a creator who brings their own audience and wants API-driven launches.
  5. Do not use: Pons V1 (launchEnabled()==false), NOXA Fun on Robinhood (launchEnabled()==false, 0 launches/24h), GIWA MARKETS on Robinhood (no creator fee share at all — 1% goes to $GIWA burn/treasury; brand-confusion project), Clanker (deployed but not documented, ~$1.5k fees/day), Zora (not found on chain).

Per $1M of trading volume, creator revenue: Bags $10,000 · Pons V2 $7,000 (+ tax) · PAIR $7,000 · Bankr $6,650 · o1 $5,000 · LetsCash $5,000–$14,500 depending on tax config · pools.trade ≈$500.

2. Venue profiles

2.1 Pons V2 — https://www.ponsfamily.com/launchpad, docs https://docs.ponsfamily.com/docs/v2 (official, observed 2026-09-06)

  • Model: bonding curve → automatic graduation into a Uniswap v4 full-range pool owned by a locker. "A launch no longer starts life as a pool. It starts on a curve that holds the entire supply, and the pool is created only at graduation." Live since 2026-08-03 (DefiLlama adapter start).
  • Supply & split (onchain, getLaunchConfig(0) on factory 0x7eD598BcEf8bd9Edd8C97A195C6d13f40801EC7e, and PoolGraduated events): supply 1,000,000,000 (18 dec); feeBps 100; phantomQuote 1.68 ETH; graduationThreshold 4.2 ETH; tick spacing 200; only one launch config (launchConfigCount()==1). Docs formula "reserved = supply × phantomQuote ÷ (phantomQuote + threshold)" → 285.7M held back, 714.3M (71.4%) sold on the curve. Observed graduation event (token 0xb707…3197): 204,081,633 tokens (20.4%) + 4.2000 ETH into the v4 position; the locker's lockedTokenSupply(token) = 81,632,653 (8.16%) stays in PonsV2LaunchLocker (so the pool is seeded at the curve's final price and the remainder is locked, not sold). No creator/dev allocation. Start FDV = 1.68 ETH ≈ $4.2k (GeckoTerminal shows fresh curves at $4.1k FDV); graduation FDV ≈ 5.88 ETH / 285.7M × 1e9 = 20.6 ETH ≈ $51k.
  • Quote assets: ETH (11,005 of 26,711 launches/24h), USDG 0x5fc5…d168 (6,496; threshold 8,090 USDG, seen as 8.09e-9 in 18-dec decode), and approved stock tokens as custom pairs (e.g. 0xd060…9eec threshold 41.6 units ×1,408 launches; 0x4a0e…5eea 72.2 ×1,259; 0x117c…4c0c 10.9 ×628; 0x1b0e…153e 369 ×572). A sampled fresh curve (0x3e60…1ff6) showed phantomQuote = 0.4 × graduationThreshold (4.4586 vs 11.146) — the same 1.68/4.2 ratio, i.e. every pair keeps the 71.4/20.4/8.2 split.
  • Graduated pool: Uniswap v4 PoolManager 0x8366a39CC670B4001A1121B8F6A443A643e40951, pool key fee 0 / tickSpacing 200 / hook PonsV2MemeHook 0xE5e702641Ea86F4ae6cC3cDaeD2B886f976Be044 (verified). "The pool itself is set up to charge no fee of its own" — the hook charges instead. LP NFT held by PonsV2LaunchLocker 0x267444D099b10fB5Ed7c3Cc7B7c767AdcA574952 (verified; isLocked, lockedPositions) — "locked and never comes back out… Not the creator, and not pons." Position NFT positionId 1,947,236 on PositionManager 0x58daec3116aae6D93017bAAea7749052E8a04fA7. If the auto-graduation runs out of gas anyone can call createGraduatedPool(token).
  • Fees (onchain, hook currentFeePolicy()): hookFeeBps 100 (1%) on curve and pool trades; protocolFeeShareBps 3000 (30%); buybackBurnBps 5000 (if the creator enables buyback, 50% of the creator remainder → buys and burns/vests the meme token, i.e. 35/35/30); maxInternalPriceImpactBps 300. Protocol fee recipient 0x263ed295dAFaE1d9AAdD6E56c4B6F9f38eE019Dd. Creator tax: optional, immutable, capped at maxCreatorTaxBps() = 1000 (10%); the sampled fresh curve had creatorTaxBps = 100; adapter breakdown: "Creator Tax: taxes collected from creators (if enabled) on curve swaps" → 100% to creator, curve phase only. DefiLlama: "Part of (30% for most pools) … goes to the protocol"; "70% when buyback is disabled, 35% when enabled" to creators. Protocol side: 80% of protocol fees → TWAP buyback-and-burn of PONS, 20% ops (docs V1 page).
  • Launch fee: launchFee() = 0.0005 ETH (≈$1.24). Hidden cost: none besides gas (~0.5–1.35 gwei).
  • Dev buy: "Launch & Buy Router" 0xe33E9E479dF8802cb0866d5d05258bEc4cF62948 executes launch + first buy atomically; the launching address and the creator fee recipient are exempt from the snipe tax; creator may whitelist up to 32 extra addresses at launch.
  • Anti-snipe: opening buy tax "starts at 99% of a buy and decays exponentially to zero across the first 5 seconds… near 25% one second in and around 3% at two seconds"; onchain snipeTaxSeconds() = 3. "What is collected is not burned. It joins the launch's trading fee and is distributed the same way" — i.e. sniper tax feeds the creator/protocol split.
  • Claim: pull. Fees accrue in PonsV2FeeEscrow 0xd3AFEB2a57f70eF218Aa82451c51B2fb0416Ac9e (verified) per asset; claim() for ETH, claimToken(address) for USDG/stock pairs; post-graduation pool fees are swept by feeSweepOperator 0x49BbF2b70955Fb3a106e084D4BFDa92d334573d2 (PoolFeesSwept(poolId, protocolAmount, buybackAmount, creatorAmount, tokensLocked)). Creator can change only the fee-recipient wallet and the buyback toggle. Buyback vault 0x42df2a798f82289E177311362e8f5ccC45c1219c vests bought-back tokens over five years split creator/protocol. UI: ponsfamily.com/launchpad → Explore / Analytics / Create / Profile.
  • Other: V1→V2 community migration contract exists; CTO (community takeover) via form for abandoned tokens; PONS token 0x39dbed3a2bd333467115de45665cc57f813c4571 (FDV ≈ $630M on GeckoTerminal) is the platform token. Pons V2 curves appear on GeckoTerminal as dex pons-v2 (curve stage) and pons-v2-dex (graduated).
  • Risk notes: 26.7k launches/day means brutal noise — the graduated set is <3%; USDG- and stock-paired launches are a differentiator (NEKO/NVDA, AMZN/CRC, SPY/NOTHING all graduated this week).
