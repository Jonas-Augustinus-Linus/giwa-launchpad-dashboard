GIWA Lab Constitution

This repository is a research and testnet-readiness workspace. It is not a trading system, token issuer, launchpad, exchange, endorsement, or production deployment authority.

Non-negotiable boundaries

  1. Research only. Every ecosystem record and generated observation must preserve research_only=true, strategy_consumption_allowed=false, and order_input=false.
  2. Read-only by default. Repository tooling may query an allowlisted set of public JSON-RPC read methods. It must not sign, send, relay, simulate for execution, or request wallet secrets.
  3. Testnet is not production. GIWA Sepolia tokens have no represented value. No testnet metric may be described as users, adoption, liquidity, market demand, or mainnet readiness.
  4. Fail closed. Tooling must stop if the chain ID is not exactly 91342, an endpoint is unsafe, evidence is missing, or a fact cannot be classified confidently.
  5. Human authority. Adding any write path, contract deployment command, private key, funded wallet, mainnet endpoint, liquidity action, market promotion, or external publication requires a separate, explicit review and approval. A code merge is not that approval.
  6. No implied affiliation or listing. Third-party projects must be labelled as such. GIWA, Dunamu, Upbit, GASOK, or testnet participation must never be represented as a token listing, endorsement, selection, or investment signal without direct official evidence.
  7. Provenance over convenience. Current claims must point to registered evidence and retain their observed date. UNKNOWN is preferable to inference.
  8. No secrets. Secrets, private keys, mnemonics, access tokens, funded account details, and user-identifying telemetry do not belong in source, logs, fixtures, screenshots, or research.

These rules have precedence over convenience, examples, roadmaps, and automation in this repo.
